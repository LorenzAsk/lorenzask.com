Privacy

Privacy.

This is a personal website. It has no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't try to find out who you are. This page explains the little data that does pass through it.

Who is responsible

Lorenz Ask, as a private person, is responsible for this website and the data described here. You can reach me at lorenzask@kvantra.ai. That address belongs to Kvantra, so emails to it are delivered and stored by Kvantra's email provider.

When you visit

Hosting. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, Vercel's servers process your IP address and basic technical details, such as your browser and the time of the request, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. Vercel keeps these logs only for as long as it needs them to run the service, as described in Vercel's privacy policy.

Fonts. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

When you write to me

The form on the Contact page doesn't send anything to this site. It opens your own email app with your message filled in, and nothing leaves your device until you press send there.

If you write to me, I use your email address and message only to reply. I don't add you to any list. I keep our correspondence for as long as the conversation is relevant, and no longer than two years after our last contact, unless you ask me to delete it sooner.

Legal basis

Serving the site and answering your emails is based on legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): a website can't be delivered without processing a visitor's IP address, and a reply needs your email.

Data outside Europe

Vercel and Google are based in the United States, so your data may be processed outside the EU/EEA. Both companies take part in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and Vercel also uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses to protect data transferred out of the EEA.

Your rights

You have the right to see the personal data I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict how it is used, and to receive it in a portable format. Because I rely on legitimate interest, you also have the right to object to the processing at any time. To use any of these rights, email lorenzask@kvantra.ai.

If you think I've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to Datatilsynet, the Norwegian Data Protection Authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date below.
