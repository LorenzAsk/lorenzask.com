About

Zurich.
Ålesund.
Full speed.

Raised between two countries, shaped by the Arctic, and building ever since.

I was born in Zurich and grew up on skis. Ten years in the city, every winter in the Swiss Alps, then a move to Ålesund, two years back in Zurich, and finally Ålesund for good in 9th grade.

I started investing at 12. Watching the people around me invest without a method made it obvious what was missing: a consistent way to judge a company against the ones it actually competes with.

After high school I went straight into the military as an idrettssoldat: a year north of the Arctic Circle, near Lofoten, where I was promoted to ledende menig. In April 2025, in the military sauna, the idea for Kvantra arrived. It has been my life ever since.

In August 2025 I started running. I'm new to running, but not to endurance: now I'm training for the Oslo–Bergen ultra. In between, I travel when I can, and I'll solve a Rubik's cube for anyone who asks. My best is 18 seconds.

Full speed ahead.
