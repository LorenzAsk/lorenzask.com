I grew up in Zurich, Switzerland, and started investing at 12. Watching people around me try to invest without a method made it obvious what was missing: a consistent way to judge a company against the ones it actually competes with.

That missing piece became KvantraRank. I co-founded Kvantra to put it in the hands of retail investors: the same numbers a professional would see, without the noise.
