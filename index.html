  1. Born in Zurich

    Ten years in the city, and every winter on skis in the Swiss Alps. I've skied since I could stand.

  2. First move north

    Two years on the west coast of Norway.

  3. Started investing

    Watching the people around me invest without a method made it obvious what was missing.

  4. Back again

    Two more years in Zurich.

  5. Home for good

    Settled in Ålesund in 9th grade and finished high school here.

  6. Idrettssoldat

    Straight from high school into the military: a year north of the Arctic Circle, near Lofoten. Promoted to ledende menig.

  7. An idea in the sauna

    The idea for Kvantra came in the military sauna. It has been my life ever since.

  8. New to running, not to endurance

    Started running, and kept going. Now training for the Oslo–Bergen ultra.

  9. Ahead

    Building Kvantra, learning to code, and putting in the kilometres.
