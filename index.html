02 — KvantraRank Drag the dial
80 KvantraRank

A score of 80% means a stock ranks higher than that share of its peers.

One number. Three reasons.

Every stock gets a score from 0 to 100, ranked only against its real peers. Open it, and you see exactly where it comes from.

  • Q

    Quality

    Profitability, cash generation and balance-sheet strength.

  • V

    Value

    How cheaply a company is priced relative to its fundamentals.

  • M

    Momentum

    Whether it has outperformed its peers, including the trend in analyst expectations.
