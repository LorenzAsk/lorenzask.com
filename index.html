Startup

kvantra.ai

Many young investors pick their own stocks, but the numbers they need are locked behind barriers, from complex terminals to high prices. So they fill the gap with tips and social media. Kvantra is a stock research platform that makes the numbers easy to use, in one workflow with a screener, portfolio tracking, watchlists and KvantraRank, our factor score that gives each of 60 000+ stocks on 66 exchanges a score from 0 to 100.

We’re now building AI-native features on top of that foundation, so analysis that used to take hours takes seconds. Next is an AI macro analyst that reads around 300 000 news articles a day and shows which of your stocks are affected, including second- and third-order effects.
